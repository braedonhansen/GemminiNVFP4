**Decisions this implements**
- **Build:** NVFP4-only config (option iii). Swap `NvFp4Mesh` in at `MeshWithDelays.scala:187` for the NVFP4 config only. MX configs must still elaborate unchanged.
- **Scale stage:** exact, fixed-function, **inside `AccumulatorMem`**, replacing `applyE9M0Scale` in the NVFP4 config.
  1. Stage 0: sA·sB (E4M3 × E4M3: a 4×4-bit significand multiply plus an exponent add).
  2. Stage 1: × the 13-bit lane sum, giving up to 21 bits.
  3. Stage 2: pack to FP32 with no rounding.
  4. Then the FP32 accumulate add.
  - Raise `acc_latency` as needed, and re-check the RAW hazard handling (`AccumulatorMem.scala:118-120`).
- **Scale memory:** `ScaleFactorMem` outputs **raw E4M3 bytes**, sA for the row and sB for the column.
- **Accumulator:** FP32 accumulator, FP32 mvout.
- **Blocks:** `scaleSize = 16` and DIM = 16. Replace the hardcoded 32s (`ScaleFactorMem.scala:196`, `LoopMatmul.scala:1106`) with `scaleSize`.
- **Tensor scale:** applied at mvout via the existing CONFIG_EX `acc_scale` (`out = acc · (tA·tB/4)`), in the refmodel's float32 order.
