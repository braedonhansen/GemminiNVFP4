# Brief: M6.5, end-to-end NVFP4 matmuls across multiple tiles, in Spike and Verilator (first working version)

Follow `AGENT_LOOP.md` (including the rule that a check must fail when it checked nothing). Read first:
- `docs/M6.3C-integration.md` (FP32 store addressing: chunk stride, j-group, row tile and row step, all derived but **not yet run** for multi-tile)

**Goal**: NVFP4 matmuls larger than one 32×32 quad tile and longer than K = 64 run **bit-exact against the refmodel in both Spike (`--extension=gemmini_nvfp4`) and Verilator (`NvFp4GemminiRocketConfig`)**. This is the project's "first working version".

**Shapes** (all multiples of 32 in M and N and of 16 in K). Keep the Verilator wall time reasonable: estimate it from the 2,555-cycle 32×32×64 case, and drop the largest shape if it needs more than ~30 min.
1. M=64, N=64, K=128: 2×2 tiles, 8 K-blocks
2. M=32, N=96, K=64: tiles along N only
3. M=96, N=32, K=256: tiles along M only, long K
4. M=64, N=64, K=512: long K across tiles (the accumulation-order stress)
- Every case keeps the zero/subnormal scale forcing and has outputs that are sensitive to the -0 fix. Each case uses its own seed.
