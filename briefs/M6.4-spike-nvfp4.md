- the E2M1 products and exact integer column sums per 16-element K block (no MX precision ramp)
- the E4M3 sA·sB × sum scale stage (exact)
- the FP32 accumulate in the refmodel's float32 order, with the first write = +0 + blk
- the tensor factor at mvout (RNE)
- the FP32 store layout, matching the RTL

The `nvfp4_e2e` test must then pass in Spike exactly as it does in Verilator. The default (MX) libgemmini build must be unchanged.
